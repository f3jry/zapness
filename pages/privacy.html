Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zapness ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you play the Zapness Discord Activity application ("Service").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Zapness is designed with privacy by default. We do not operate central database servers that collect, store, or sell your personal information, messages, or Discord account passwords.

2. Data Used During Gameplay

To provide multiplayer gameplay through the Discord Embedded App SDK, the following transient data is processed locally on your client:

  • Discord Channel & Instance Identifiers: The game reads your Discord Voice Channel ID and Activity Instance ID solely to allow players in the same voice call to connect to each other automatically without manually entering room codes. This data is kept in memory during the match and is not stored externally.
  • Peer-to-Peer Networking (WebRTC): In online multiplayer matches, gameplay state (player positions, inputs, projectile actions) is transmitted directly between players' devices via WebRTC. Standard ICE candidates and connection metadata are exchanged through public WebRTC signaling to establish the connection.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with the following third-party infrastructure:

  • Discord: The game runs inside Discord's Embedded App SDK environment and is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: The game client assets (HTML, WASM, JavaScript) are statically hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical access data (such as IP addresses and user agents) in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • PeerJS Signaling: WebRTC connection establishment uses public STUN/signaling servers to negotiate direct peer-to-peer data channels.

4. Data Retention

Because Zapness does not maintain accounts or store personal data on remote servers, we retain no persistent personal records.

5. Children's Privacy

Zapness does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age required in your jurisdiction).

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the official project repository: github.com/f3jry/zapness.
